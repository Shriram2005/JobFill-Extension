Data Privacy & AI Disclosure

JobFill is designed for privacy:
• Local First: Obvious personal and contact fields are matched locally on your device with 0 API calls.
• Minimal AI Transmission: Only unmatched form labels and questions are sent to the Google Gemini API. Sensitive credentials (passwords, SSNs) are strictly filtered out.
• No Telemetry: No analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party ad servers are ever contacted.
• Never Auto-Submits: JobFill never clicks Submit or Apply buttons. You always retain final control.
